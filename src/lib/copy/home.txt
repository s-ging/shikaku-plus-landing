[about.title]
About Shikaku+

[about.body]
Shikaku+ is a cooperative and competitive puzzle platform built on the Nikoli puzzle, Shikaku.

The core mechanic is simple: partition a grid into rectangles so each rectangle contains exactly one number, and the rectangle’s area matches that number.

# ─── Questions ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# "Q: " starts a question, "A: " its answer. Add or remove pairs freely.

[faq.title]
Questions

[faq]
Q: Is Shikaku+ free?
A: Yes. Free to play, with no ads.

Q: Can I play with friends?
A: Yes. Send a Help Me! link to finish a board together, or race each other in Shikaku+ VS.

Q: Does it work offline?
A: Yes. The Android app plays without a connection.

Q: How big is it?
A: About 9.7MB to download from Google Play. In the browser there is nothing to install.

Q: Do I need an account?
A: No, you can play as a guest. An account keeps your badges, stats and player tag.

Q: Is there an iPhone app?
A: Not yet. On an iPhone, play in the browser at play.shikaku.plus.

Q: What is Shikaku?
A: A Japanese logic puzzle by Nikoli. Shikaku (四角) means square. You split the grid into rectangles, each holding exactly one number equal to its area.

Q: What is Hokan?
A: Hokan (補完) means completion. Part of the board comes filled in, and you fit a tray of box sizes into the gaps.
